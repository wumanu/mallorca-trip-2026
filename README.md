# Mallorca · 19–25 October 2026

[Open the research guide](https://wumanu.github.io/mallorca-trip-2026/) · [Open Ahmad’s trip package](https://wumanu.github.io/mallorca-trip-2026/ahmad.html)

This repository is the canonical home for the public HTML pages: `index.html` is the full research guide and `ahmad.html` is the visual trip proposal. Private notes, booking documents and personal records remain in Obsidian and are not published here.

The research guide combines an interactive map with beaches, snorkeling and freediving, wind sports, padel/pickleball, restaurants, bars, nightlife, accommodation and towns. Categories toggle independently; Freedive Mallorca, Kitesurf Mallorca and Cuatro Vientos remain starred on the map. The initial view focuses on Port de Pollença, with optional detailed street tiles and an offline coastline.

Accommodation research covers 30 ranked northern stays and 13 neighborhoods, with room arrangements, dated availability and prices, embedded photos, parking, activity drives and direct booking/review links. The earlier 34-property island-wide research is retained as historical data. Torre Playa is booked for 2 adults, 19–25 October 2026: six nights, two bedrooms and sea view, €1,271.70 non-refundable. Earlier tourist-tax allowances remain estimates until verified against the booking documents.

The towns category compares 90 places: all 53 municipalities plus 37 coastal towns, districts and villages. Each has a photograph, population with scope/year, swimming and evening details, and a ranking explanation. The racket-sports category covers 66 researched venues, including three confirmed pickleball facilities, with sport/area/rank filters and booking sources. Visitor access and October court availability are not guaranteed.

Wingfoil findings retain the 30 September review analysis of all 596 Google entries and 166 Tripadvisor reviews across the three main schools. Kitesurf Mallorca and Cuatro Vientos are the finalists; the guide distinguishes teaching evidence, complaints and owner replies from quoted offers. Rankings reflect this trip’s priorities, not platform ranking or a statistical consensus. Each place links its sources and dated caveats.

Download either HTML file and open it in a browser. Photos, scripts and the basic map are embedded; optional street tiles and external links require internet. Edit and publish the HTML from this repository rather than maintaining duplicate copies in Obsidian.
